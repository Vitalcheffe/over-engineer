# LWR — first reading

Lighthill-Whitham (1955), Richards (1956):
- ∂ρ/∂t + ∂(ρv)/∂x = 0
- v = v(ρ) (equilibrium relation)

Greenshields (1935):
- v(ρ) = v_max (1 − ρ/ρ_max)
- q(ρ) = v_max (ρ − ρ²/ρ_max) parabola, peaks at ρ_max/2

Shock speed: w = (q₂−q₁)/(ρ₂−ρ₁).  free→jam → upstream (w<0).
